Frequently Asked Questions

DPF FAQ's

Below are popular questions regarding Diesel Particulate Filters and why regular cleaning is essential.

Diesel Particulate Filters (DPFs) were introduced to meet ever-tightening emissions regulations and are fitted to almost all modern diesel cars. They trap and remove soot from exhaust gases. Over time, soot and ash build up, causing the filter to choke—especially when driving short city journeys at low speeds. Professional DPF cleaning flushes out this trapped residue, restoring airflow and preventing expensive catalytic damage.

If you drive a diesel car and notice any of the following symptoms, your DPF needs immediate attention:
• The orange DPF warning light is illuminated on your dashboard
• Significant loss of engine power, limp mode, or poor acceleration
• The automatic start-stop system has stopped working
• Unusually high fuel consumption (poor MPG)
• The radiator cooling fans run constantly even on short drives
