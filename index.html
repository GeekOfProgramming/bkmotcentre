About BK
MOT CENTRE

We at BK Mot Centre have 40 years combined experience in the automotive industry. We aim to provide quick, reliable service at competitive prices from our Smethwick garage with easy main road access.

We specialise in MOT testing and comprehensive vehicle servicing for all makes and models. If you need a trusted regular mechanic, our certified technicians are on hand to keep your car performing at its best.

We also specialise in Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) Cleaning — helping you avoid costly replacements if the DPF warning light is illuminated, engine power is reduced, or stop-start functionality is disabled.
